# Behavioral: Ownership & Accountability
# Core Principle: STAR Method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

## Questions:
1. Describe a time you took ownership of a project that was outside your direct responsibility. Why did you step in?
2. Tell me about a situation where you identified a significant technical risk that others missed. What action did you take to mitigate it?
3. Give an example of a mistake you made that impacted a timeline or deliverable. How did you communicate this and correct the course?
4. How do you handle a situation where a teammate is not meeting their commitments? Provide a concrete example and the outcome.

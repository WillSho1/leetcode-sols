# 0002-star-technical-challenge.md
Reference: STAR Method Framework

## Goal
Structure a STAR response for a complex technical challenge you faced.

## Questions
1. S/T: Briefly describe a technical situation where you were under pressure or faced a scaling blocker. What was the goal?
2. A: Detail the specific steps *you* took to troubleshoot and fix it. Mention tools used (e.g., profilers, logs, specific code changes).
3. R: What was the measurable outcome? (Performance improvement, downtime reduction, money saved).
4. Reflection: If you could go back, what one thing would you do differently to prevent the situation?

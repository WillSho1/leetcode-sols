# 0010 - Leadership & Ownership (STAR Method)

[Resource: STAR Method for Behavioral Interviews](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/the-star-method-for-behavioral-interviews/)

## Context
Employers want to see that you take initiative and own a problem from start to finish, even if it's not strictly "your job."

## Questions

1. **The Story:** Provide a specific situation where you noticed a team-wide problem or deficiency and took the lead on solving it.
2. **The "Task":** What was the expectation of you vs. the actual responsibility you took on?
3. **The "Action":** Detail the specific steps you took to align stakeholders and get the fix implemented.
4. **The "Result":** What were the measurable outcomes? (e.g., % reduction in bugs, time saved across the team, or a specific process improvement).
5. **Reflection:** If you were to do it again, what would you change about how you communicated your leadership in that scenario?
